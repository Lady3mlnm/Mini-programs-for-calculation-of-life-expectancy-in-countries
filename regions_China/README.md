This is a script used by me for creation of tables and maps about life expectancy in China for the Wikipedia page "[List of Chinese administrative divisions by life expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Chinese_administrative_divisions_by_life_expectancy)" and its [Russian analogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Продолжительность_жизни_в_регионах_Китая). The data is taken from the science paper "[Healthy China: A Projection of Life Expectancy Based on the Global Burden of Disease Study 2019 — China, 1990–2030](https://weekly.chinacdc.cn/en/article/doi/10.46234/ccdcw2023.008)".

Use online service [MapCharts](https://www.mapchart.net/china.html) to create maps.

This script is outdated. If you search a tempate for creation of a new script, consider scripts for another country.
