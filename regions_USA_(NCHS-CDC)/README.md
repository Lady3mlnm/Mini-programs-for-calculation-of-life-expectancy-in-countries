This is collection of scripts used by me for creation of tables, charts and maps about life expectancy in the United States for the Wikipedia page "[List of U.S. states and territories by life expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_and_territories_by_life_expectancy)" and its [Russian analogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Продолжительность_жизни_в_штатах_США). These data were manually copied from PDF-reports published every year by the [National Center for Health Statistics / CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/fastats/life-expectancy.htm).

Use online service [MapCharts](https://www.mapchart.net/usa.html) to create maps.

These scripts can be outdated. If you search a tempate for creation of a new script, I recommend to consider scripts from other folders.
