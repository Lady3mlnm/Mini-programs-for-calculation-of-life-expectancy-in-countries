These are scripts used by me for creation of tables and maps about life expectancy in India for the Wikipedia page "[List of Indian states by life expectancy at birth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indian_states_by_life_expectancy_at_birth)" and its [Russian analogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Продолжительность_жизни_в_штатах_и_территориях_Индии).

The data from the "[National Family Health Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Family_Health_Survey)" (NFHS) are taken from the science paper "[Impact of COVID-19 on subnational variations in life expectancy and life disparity at birth in India: evidence from NFHS and SRS data](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10476359/)".

The data from the "Sample Registration System" (SRS) are taken from the site of [RCCGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Registrar_General_and_Census_Commissioner_of_India).

Use online service [MapCharts](https://www.mapchart.net/india.html) to create maps.

These scripts are outdated. If you search a tempate for creation of a new script, consider scripts for another country.
