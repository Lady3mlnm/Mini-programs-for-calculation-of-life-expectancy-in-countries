This is collection of scripts used by me for creation of tables, charts and maps about life expectancy in the United States for the Wikipedia page "[List of U.S. states and territories by life expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_and_territories_by_life_expectancy)" and its [Russian analogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Продолжительность_жизни_в_штатах_США). The data is taken from the [US Mortality DataBase](https://usa.mortality.org/) that was afterwards moved to the [Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/19WYUX).

Use online service [MapCharts](https://www.mapchart.net/usa.html) to create maps.

Before running the scripts, download data (131 Mb) from the database and unpack them to the folder `data`.
