This is a script used by me for creation of tables about life expectancy in Finland for the Wikipedia page "[List of Finnish regions by life expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Finnish_regions_by_life_expectancy)" and its [Russian analogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Продолжительность_жизни_в_областях_Финляндии). The data is taken from the [Statistics Finland website](https://pxdata.stat.fi/PXWeb/pxweb/en/StatFin/StatFin__kuol/statfin_kuol_pxt_12an.px).
