This folder contains raw data taken from the World Health Organization > the [Global Health Observatory](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/themes/topics/indicator-groups/indicator-group-details/GHO/life-expectancy-and-healthy-life-expectancy).<br>The files have to be renamed to
- Life expectancy at birth -countries.csv
- Life expectancy at birth -global.csv
- Life expectancy at birth -regions.csv
- Life expectancy at birth -income_groups.csv
- Healthy life expectancy (HALE) at birth -countries.csv
- Healthy life expectancy (HALE) at birth -global.csv
- Healthy life expectancy (HALE) at birth -regions.csv
- Healthy life expectancy (HALE) at birth -income_groups.csv

Creation of the interactive SVG bubble charts are also required data for number of population from the [United Nation](https://population.un.org/wpp/downloads?folder=Standard%20Projections&group=Most%20used).<br>
The file has to be renamed to
- UN_demographic_indicators.xlsx
