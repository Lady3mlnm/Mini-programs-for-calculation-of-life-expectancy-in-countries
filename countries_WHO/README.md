This is collection of scripts used by me for creation of tables, charts, barcharts, and interactive SVG bubble charts about life expectancy and healthy life expectancy according to estimate of the World Health Organization. The data is taken from the [Global Health Observatory](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/themes/topics/indicator-groups/indicator-group-details/GHO/life-expectancy-and-healthy-life-expectancy). Data for number of population, used in the bubble charts, is taken from the [United Nation](https://population.un.org/wpp/downloads?folder=Standard%20Projections&group=Most%20used). Folders for output of charts of LE and HALE development are not shown here - they are autocteated during the according script work.
