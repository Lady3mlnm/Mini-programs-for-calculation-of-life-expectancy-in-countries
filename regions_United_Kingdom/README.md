This is a script used by me for creation of tables about life expectancy in the United Kingdom for the Wikipedia page "[List of British regions by life expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_British_regions_by_life_expectancy)" and its [Russian analogue](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Продолжительность_жизни_в_регионах_Великобритании). The data is taken from the [website of the Office for National Statistics](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/healthandsocialcare/healthandlifeexpectancies/bulletins/lifeexpectancyforlocalareasoftheuk/between2001to2003and2022to2024).
