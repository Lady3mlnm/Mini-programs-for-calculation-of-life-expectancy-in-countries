These are scripts used by me for creation tables and maps about life expectancy in countries according to estimate of the World Bank Group. The data is taken from the database: [total](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN), [male](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.MA.IN), [female](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.FE.IN).

**Warning**: these scripts are already outdated, just quickly adapted for 2024. In particular, JSONs for 2024 maps were not tested with the current version of [MapCharts](https://www.mapchart.net), and I'm not sure in their correct display. I consider the current data for 2024 to be unreliable - it will most likely be corrected soon. If you are looking script exaples for creation tables and maps for Wikipedia, pay attention to the neighboring folders.
